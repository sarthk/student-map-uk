Transparent by design

What Students Map UK actually knows

Useful places, fetched when you ask

A postcode is converted to a coordinate by postcodes.io. The app then asks an OpenStreetMap Overpass service for selected, student-useful map features within your chosen radius. The timestamp shown after a search is the OpenStreetMap database snapshot used for that query.

What “nearby” means

Distances are straight-line estimates from the postcode coordinate, not walking routes or journey times. A river, railway, campus entrance or road layout can make the real journey longer. Directions open on OpenStreetMap so you can check the route.

What can be missing or wrong

OpenStreetMap is maintained by a global community. Coverage varies, names and opening hours may be stale, and a business can close before the map is updated. “No results” never means “none exist”. Check important details with the venue itself.

Privacy

This site has no accounts, advertising tracker or behavioural analytics. Direct requests disclose normal web-request details such as your IP address and browser referrer to postcodes.io, the selected OpenStreetMap query operator and the basemap provider. A postcode is sent to postcodes.io. Its coordinate—or your approximate coordinate after browser permission—is sent to one OpenStreetMap query service at a time. Your temporary “My list” stays in this tab and disappears when it closes.

Scope and safety

The discovery map works around active UK postcodes, including Scotland and Northern Ireland; map coverage is not guaranteed. It does not verify businesses, show live capacity or accommodation availability, provide medical advice, or rate neighbourhood safety. In an emergency call 999; for non-emergency NHS help use official NHS services.

Open data, with limits

Place data is © OpenStreetMap contributors and available under the ODbL. Public Overpass services are best-effort and may occasionally be busy. The app retries another public endpoint before showing an error.
